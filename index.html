Cover Fire Arena

Duck behind walls and crates. Enemies you can’t see can’t be shot, and they can’t see you.

The head picks both codes and opens the match first. Players then join with the player code.
Move: WASD or left thumb. Aim: mouse or right thumb (hold to fire).
R reloads. Keys 1 to 5 or Q switch weapons. Shift sprints.
The head starts a timed 3 minute match with the Start match button.
